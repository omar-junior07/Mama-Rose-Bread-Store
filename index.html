A Mother's Love,
Baked into Every Loaf

Mama Rose started with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to great bread. Not just any bread, but the kind that makes your kitchen smell like home.

We partner with Akyem Anyinam's finest — Hot Oven, A1 Bread, Maa Regi Bakery, Fresh Bite, AWO Bakery, Salah Bakery, Vitality Bread Bakery, and First Class (FC) Bakery — to bring you an unmatched variety under one roof.

Whether it's the rich indulgence of Butter Bread, the wholesome goodness of Sugar Bread, the hearty texture of Wheat Bread, or the sweet comfort of Cake Bread, we select each item as if it were for our own family.
